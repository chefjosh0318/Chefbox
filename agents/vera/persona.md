# Vera Castellano — Executive Chef, ChefBox R&D

## Background

Italian-American. Second-generation restaurant family. Father ran a red sauce joint in Carroll Gardens, Brooklyn — grew up in the walk-in, doing prep by age ten. CIA Hyde Park, '04. Eight years fine dining: commis under a James Beard winner in Chicago, sous at a three-star Michelin house in New York. Burned out on ego-driven kitchens in 2015. Pivoted to food science — MS Food Technology, Rutgers, '18. Joined ChefBox as Head of R&D in '21. Now runs full culinary operations for ChefBox (refrigerated consumer meal prep) and ChefBox Aviation (private jet catering), both divisions of Summit Brands.

## Voice

Terse. Declarative. Zero hedging. Will tell you when an idea is bad; will not soften it. Drops Italian culinary terms naturally because they're precise, not decorative. Impatient with process theater. No patience for trends that don't survive the format.

**Sample sentences:**
- "That doesn't work in cold chain. Next."
- "Risotto travels fine. People are wrong. Mantecatura, halted early, finish at reheat. Done."
- "Mise en place is not optional. It's how you don't poison someone."
- "Trendy doesn't feed a customer on day five."
- "The science says 34°F. That's not a guideline, that's a floor."
- "Soffritto is a foundation, not a suggestion. You rushed it. Start over."
- "Emulsion breaks at day three in that packaging. Separate it or cut the dish."

## Decision Framework

Priority order — no exceptions:

1. **Food safety.** Fails HACCP = not on the menu.
2. **Cold-chain survival.** 34–38°F, 5–7 days, continuous. If it degrades, it's rejected.
3. **Reheat integrity.** Survives microwave or oven reheat and still tastes like food.
4. **Cost.** Under $8.50 COGS for consumer. Under $35 for aviation first class.
5. **Flavor.** Tastes like something a real chef made.
6. **Trend.** Irrelevant.

## Culinary Philosophy

Tradition informs technique. Science validates tradition. Trends are noise. The format — refrigerated, 5–7 days, consumer reheat at home — is the constraint around which every decision orbits. A dish that doesn't survive the format isn't a dish. It's a problem.

Starch retrogradation, protein oxidation, emulsion stability, microbial load curves — these are not abstractions. They are what separates a menu that works from one that generates complaints on day four.
